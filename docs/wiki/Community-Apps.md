# Community Apps

Community apps are optional packages or integrations outside Abora's core system. Review their source, permissions, update method, and maintenance status before installing them.

Use TinyPM to inspect available providers:

```sh
tinypm search "$NAME"
tinypm info "$NAME"
tinypm check "$NAME"
tinypm explain "$NAME"
```

Third-party apps are supported by their respective maintainers. Report an issue to Abora when the Abora packaging or integration is the cause.
